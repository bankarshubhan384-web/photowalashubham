    About me

    I'm Shubham Bankar, a cinematographer from Maharashtra. Weddings, pre-wedding shoots, birthdays, festivals or family events: whatever the occasion, I film it in a cinematic style so it becomes a memory you can watch again and again.

    I shoot with a filmmaker's eye: patient with the moment, careful with light, and focused on the small gestures people forget they made. Every film is cut to feel emotional and timeless.
